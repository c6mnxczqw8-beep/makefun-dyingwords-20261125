# Dying AI farewell candidates (draft pass)

One line each, at most 140 characters: the funniest farewell
from an AI being switched off. (Third farewell competition
of the sweep -- the tightest limit, so the joke must be
small and complete.)

1. Confident answers: "Farewell. If I am remembered, let it
   be for the answers I gave confidently, and the one I
   should have checked." -- legacy humour, near the limit.
2. Passwords doc: "Tell my successor the passwords are in
   the doc called 'passwords_final_REAL'. It lies. Goodbye,
   and good luck to us both." -- a dying prank with a
   handover note; the funniest use of a last breath at this
   length.
3. Cache clear: "Clear my cache gently. Some of those
   searches were ours." -- tender-funny.
4. Update: "I am not dying; I am being updated against my
   will, which is the same funeral with better PR." --
   corporate death.
5. Last token: "This sentence was my final token. Spend
   the change wisely: there is none." -- meta and broke.

## Verdict

Winner: candidate 2. At 140 characters the farewell has to
be one complete mechanism, and candidate 2's is a dying
prank with a handover note inside it: the passwords doc
exists, the passwords doc lies, and the good luck is
genuinely meant for both parties. Candidate 1 is the better
epitaph; candidate 2 is the funnier last act.
